# J SPA Saudi Arabic Copywriting Skill

## Editorial Tone
- Target reader: discerning woman in Al Madinah Al Munawwarah seeking private at-home spa and beauty care.
- Tone: warm, feminine, dignified, contemporary Modern Standard Arabic — clear, concise, and hospitable without forced slang or extravagant clichés.

## Hard Forbidden Terms & Patterns
- Zero Egyptian colloquialisms: `عايزة`, `النهارده`, `إيه`, `أدلع`, `بجهز`, `جربي`, `بتاع`, `كده`.
- Zero superseded group labels: `حجز جماعي`, `جلسة جماعية`, `Beauty Together`. Always use **تنسيق جلسات المناسبات** / **Occasion Experiences** and **المستفيدة 1..5**.
- Zero unverified clinical, medical, or sanitization certification claims.
- Zero unnecessary inline English inside Arabic copy unless citing formal brand marks (`J SPA`).

## Typographic & RTL Formatting Rules
1. Wrap numeric currency, duration, and phone figures inside `<bdi>` within RTL sentences (e.g. `<bdi>195 ر.س</bdi>`, `<bdi>60 دقيقة</bdi>`).
2. Prefer concise 1–2 line button labels so pills never wrap awkwardly across lines on `360px–390px` screens.
3. Keep all CTAs, error banners, empty states, WhatsApp message templates, email/SMS notices (none used), admin controls, and `<title>`/`<meta>` tags 100% consistent in tone.
