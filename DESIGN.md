# J SPA design exploration

Open `/concepts.html` on the running application. The three selectable directions are responsive, complete page studies, each covering a desktop/mobile hero, service presentation, package cards, booking step and loyalty preview. They are explicitly visual prototypes; the form does not submit a booking. Original SVG still-life, botanical, nail and product illustrations are created for this project. They do not depict real staff, customers or purchasable branded products.

## A — Botanical Ritual (selected)
Warm ivory, sage, fine borders and an arched illustrated still-life. Serif headlines are paired with quiet sans-serif utility text. A calm, legible booking card and botanical reward mark carry the visual identity into operational screens. Best match for the brief’s home-care positioning and palette; supports long Arabic text without requiring dramatic image/text overlap.

## B — Modern Blush
Blush-dominant surfaces, rounded geometry, tilted imagery, sans-serif display typography and softly sculpted cards. More contemporary and beauty-led. Strong for nail promotions, but extensive pink surfaces give massage and operational screens less visual breathing room than A.

## C — Luxury Editorial
Oversized serif typography, asymmetrical image/text composition, grayscale-muted artwork, thin rules and unequal service columns. The strongest magazine-like presence, but tightly overlapping display type needs greater content constraints and is less forgiving for bilingual copy.

## Selection and implementation
A is selected, borrowing C’s generous spacing and typographic restraint. The functional platform is maintained separately from the previews. The preview states are intentionally not live customer records. Package figures and loyalty examples are provisional business inputs, not real activity or customer balances. No invented reviews, staff photographs or popularity counts are used.

The installed 12ui instructions were reviewed. Its hosted generation workflow describes paid runs; the brief prohibits activating paid design services without approval. No paid generation was activated. These local, hand-authored studies are an alternative workflow, not a claim that 12ui generated or verified them.

## Before public launch
Commission or license professional photographs covering all service types, with documented usage rights and appropriate consent. Current original illustrations are safe placeholders but do not satisfy the brief’s final photography requirement. Confirm approved Arabic and English wording, credentials-sensitive service descriptions, brand mark ownership and final package pricing. Test the final production fonts on Arabic and English devices. Concept previews are not production acceptance evidence.
